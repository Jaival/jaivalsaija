Set up my [Kubernetes Learning Lab](https://github.com/Jaival/kubernetes-learning-lab) repo. It's a hands-on course that runs on a three-node `kind` cluster on my laptop: fourteen modules from Pods and Gateway API routing through RBAC, Helm, Prometheus and Argo CD, then a troubleshooting lab with six broken apps. The course material is the first commit. Everything after that is my own notes and exercise work, one module at a time. It ends with a capstone, `kube-tasks`, in its own repo.
